# Code of Conduct

We want EchoMere's community to be respectful, constructive, and safe.

Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, respect privacy, and avoid harassment or discriminatory language. Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security advisory channel. Maintainers will review reports in good faith and protect reporters' privacy where practical.
